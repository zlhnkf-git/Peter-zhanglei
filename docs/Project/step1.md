Aim to build a fully customizable AI interactive doll through STEM innovation practice.
Most existing AI dolls on the market have fixed appearances. They are typically plush toys embedded with AI chips that only support basic voice interaction and companionship.
I plan to combine 3D printing technology with the expandable hardware capabilities of ESP32, integrated with large language models for deep customization. Users can 3D-print the shell to create unique appearances, and tune the character personality, dialogue style and voice interaction logic via LLMs. The end result will be a robotic companion that users assemble and nurture from scratch by themselves.
Just imagine: getting to build your own robot, much like Dr. Ochanomizu creating Astro Boy — what a fascinating journey!
